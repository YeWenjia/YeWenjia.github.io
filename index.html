About me

I am a senior research associate at the University of Bristol, working with Prof. Meng Wang. Previously, I was a research fellow in the School of Computing, National University of Singapore, working with Prof. Manuel Rigger. I received my PhD from the University of Hong Kong under the supervision of Prof. Bruno C. d. S. Oliveira.

Research focus

I work on type systems for programming languages: designing them, giving them precise semantics, and proving them sound, then carrying these foundations into AI compilers, databases, probabilistic programming, and memory safety.

Current work

AI compilers
Designing a typed, staged language for TileLang and Triton, the tile-based languages used to write GPU kernels, as the start of my work on AI compilers.
Graph databases
Using verification to improve both the correctness and the efficiency of graph database systems.
Memory safety
Applying type systems and gradual typing to memory safety, modelling the boundary between safe and unsafe Rust.

  • Gradual typing

    Letting static and dynamic typing coexist in one language. I gave gradual languages a simpler, type-directed operational semantics, and extended gradual typing to polymorphism with references and to probabilistic programs.

    ECOOP 2021 ESOP 2023 OOPSLA 2023 OOPSLA 2024 JFP 2024

  • Type inference & subtyping

    Principal type inference that puts static overloading on a firm footing, and bidirectional typing for intersection subtyping in the presence of mutable references.

    PLDI 2025 OOPSLA 2024

  • Types for query languages

    Type-theoretic foundations for SQL that explain how engines convert values and why they disagree, and a flexible type discipline for path patterns in the graph query language GQL.

    ESOP 2025 OOPSLA 2025 TOPLAS 2026
